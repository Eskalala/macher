## 2. Principles
Macher is a party game for events. We process only the data needed to operate the
game. **We use no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party analytics SDKs, and
we do not sell or rent data.**

## 3. What data we process

**a) Account & profile**
Display name, chosen emoji, optional profile photo, optional short description
(bio); the crews you join.

**b) Game content (user-generated content)**
Photo and video "proofs" that you capture and post; your reactions, comments,
votes, and reports.

**c) Sign-in**
With "Sign in with Apple" we receive a pseudonymous user identifier issued by
Apple. We receive **no password**; you can hide your email address from Apple, and
the game does not need it. Anyone who only browses uses an anonymous account
without Apple sign-in.

**d) Push notifications**
If you enable notifications, we process a device token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
in order to send you messages. You can disable this at any time in the app or
system settings.

**e) Camera & microphone**
Access occurs only when you capture a proof, and only after you grant permission
in the system. Recordings are stored only once you post them.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases
- **Providing the game** (account, profile, crews, proofs, scores):
  Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the user contract).
- **Push notifications**: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), revocable at any time with
  effect for the future.
- **Security, abuse and fraud prevention, moderation of reported content**:
  Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a safe community) as well as
  Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with the Digital Services Act, to the extent
  statutory reporting and moderation obligations apply.

## 5. Recipients and processors
**Other users** see your content according to the visibility you choose: crew
proofs only your crew, publicly posted proofs everyone taking part in the
respective event.

We use the following processors (Art. 28 GDPR):
- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud
  Messaging) of Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC. Data is stored primarily in the
  EU region (`europe-west1`). A data processing agreement is in place.
- **Apple** (Sign in with Apple) for sign-in.

## 6. Transfers to third countries

## 7. Retention
We store your data for as long as your account exists. When you **delete your
account** (in Settings), your profile, your posted photos and videos, your
comments, and your crew membership are deleted server-side and the sign-in is
removed; this usually happens within a few minutes. To fulfil legal obligations
or to defend against abuse, individual log data may be retained for a shorter,
limited period.
